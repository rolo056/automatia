Questions

About done-for-you cold email.

Do you guarantee meetings?

No. Meetings depend on the list, the offer and the timing. Before the first email we agree in writing what I will measure and what would make us pause or stop.

Who owns the domains and inboxes?

You do. They are bought in your name and billed at cost. If we part ways, everything stays with you.

How soon do the first emails go out?

New domains and inboxes need about two weeks of warm-up. That time goes into building and verifying the list and writing the emails, so sending starts as soon as the inboxes are ready.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I can work behind the scenes inside your clients' accounts, under your brand. If your own sending is the problem, start with an email deliverability audit.
