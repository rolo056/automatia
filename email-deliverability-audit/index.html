What I check

Everything between your email and the inbox.

When replies drop, the copy is the last suspect. These come first.

  • Authentication on every sending domain. SPF, DKIM and DMARC, whether they align with the address you send from, and MX so replies can land.
  • Blocklists. Your domains, and the domains inside your links and tracking, checked against Spamhaus and SURBL.
  • Sending history. Sends, bounces, replies and errors for each campaign, so a drop gets a date and a cause.
  • Volume and warm-up. Emails per inbox per day, how old each domain is, and how fast it was ramped up.
  • Tracking. Whether the open-tracking pixel and link tracking are on, and what they cost you at Microsoft 365 and Proofpoint.
  • Your list. Catch-all and unverified addresses, the bounces they cause, and where the contacts came from.
  • An inbox placement test. Your real email sent to seed inboxes at Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo, to see where it lands: inbox, spam or nowhere.
