Software that removes repeated work from small businesses

Automatia Agency is an independent software studio operated by Alexander Rosales in Plattsburgh, New York. I build email infrastructure, workflow automation and fast websites for small businesses and independent professionals, and I work with the client directly rather than through account managers.

Services

Email infrastructure and outreach systems

Sending platforms, sequenced follow up with automatic reply detection, unified inboxes, bounce handling, address verification, and domain authentication with SPF, DKIM and DMARC correctly aligned.

Workflow automation and integrations

Connecting systems that do not talk to each other: APIs, webhooks, scheduled jobs, reconciliation between platforms, and removing manual re-entry of data that is already digital.

Websites and landing pages

Hand written, lightweight pages built for speed on mobile connections. Existing sites rebuilt at a fraction of their original weight, with the same content and functionality.

Data extraction and processing

Turning documents, spreadsheets and public records into structured data, including cleaning, deduplication, enrichment and delivery in the format your software will accept.

How pricing works

Every engagement is quoted in writing before any work starts. There are two ways to work together, and the client chooses which one applies.

ModelRateWhen it applies
Fixed price project Quoted per project, typically USD 100 to USD 1,500 The scope can be defined in advance. The price does not change unless the client changes the scope in writing.
Hourly USD 20 to USD 35 per hour Ongoing work, maintenance, or work whose scope cannot be fixed in advance. Hours worked are reported to the client.

Prices are quoted and invoiced in United States dollars. Payment is accepted through the freelancing platform on which the engagement was agreed, or by bank transfer. No work begins before the client has approved the written quote.

Work
