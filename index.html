Case file · client anonymized

67 domains that kept dying.

A B2B team kept replacing sending domains every few months and couldn't say why. Replies had fallen from 4–6% to under 2%.

  1. October 2025 to February 2026

    Main campaigns reply at 4.1% to 5.7%.

  2. April 15 to 28

    Bounces reach 4.23%, with two daily spikes. Replies still at 2.93%.

  3. May 1 to 14

    Replies fall to 0.44%, two weeks after the bounce spike.

  4. May 22

    Same copy, same leads, same day: new inboxes reply at 2.90%, old ones at 1.71%.

  5. October

    103 domains deleted, 45 more scheduled. Gmail placement on the newest batch drops from 20/20 to 7/21.

  • Domains wear out in about 2 to 3 monthsConfirmed
  • Complaints and bounces are what wear them outStrong evidence
  • Outlook, Yahoo and AOL filter it with any copyConfirmed
  • The tracking pixel alone triggers Microsoft 365 and ProofpointConfirmed
