Questions

Email deliverability, answered plainly.

What does an email deliverability consultant do?

Finds out why your email lands in spam and fixes it. For cold email that means checking your sending domains, inboxes and lists from the outside, testing where your email lands at Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo, and telling you in writing what to change first.

What does a cold email deliverability audit include?

SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MX on every sending domain, blocklists, bounce and reply history by campaign, sending volume per inbox, tracking settings, and an inbox placement test. You get a written report with every finding rated by how sure I am and the fixes ranked by impact. See the audit.

How much does an email deliverability audit cost?

From $350 for up to 5 sending domains. Larger setups are quoted in writing before any work starts.

Why are my cold emails going to spam?

In the audits I've run, it was rarely the copy. The usual causes are worn-out sending domains, bounces from unverified lists, too much volume per inbox and the open-tracking pixel. Read the full guide, with the numbers.

Can you fix emails going to spam in Gmail or Outlook?

I can find the cause and fix what is under your control: authentication, domains, volume, list quality and tracking. Gmail and Microsoft make the final call, so I measure placement before and after instead of promising a number.

Do you need access to my accounts?

Not to start. The first check uses only your public DNS: try it free. For the full audit I ask for access to your sending tool, and nothing is changed without your written approval.

Do you also run cold email campaigns?

Yes. I set up domains and inboxes in your name, build and verify the lists, write one checked line per contact and run the sending, with replies in your own dashboard. How it works.
